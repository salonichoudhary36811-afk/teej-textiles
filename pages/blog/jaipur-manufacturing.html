Fabric & Sourcing

Choosing the Right Fabric
for Your Collection

The fabric you choose defines the look, feel and success of your collection. Here's a practical guide to help you make informed decisions.

By Teej India Textiles | March 10, 2025 | 6 min read

Fabric is the foundation of every garment. It influences the design, fit, comfort, durability and the overall experience your customer has with your brand. Whether you are launching your first collection or expanding an existing line, understanding fabrics will help you create products that look beautiful and perform well.

01

Understand Your Collection

Start with the purpose of your collection. Is it resort wear, everyday essentials, occasion wear or something more specialised? The right fabric depends on your design, season, target market and price point.

The right fabric enhances your design and brings your vision to life.

02

Explore Common Fabrics

Here are some popular fabrics and why they are commonly chosen for women's apparel collections.

COTTON

Breathable, soft and versatile.

VISCOSE

Known for its fluid drape and comfort.

LINEN

Lightweight, natural and ideal for warm climates.

RAYON

Soft, breathable and suitable for printed styles.

03

Key Factors to Consider

When selecting a fabric, consider how it will work with your design and how your customer will experience the finished garment.

—

Comfort: Choose fabrics that feel good on the skin.

—

Drape & Structure: Ensure the fabric suits your silhouette.

—

Seasonality: Consider lighter fabrics for summer and heavier options for cooler seasons.

—

Care & Durability: Think about how the fabric will perform after repeated use.

—

Sustainability: Explore responsibly sourced and eco-conscious options.

04

Work with the Right Manufacturing Partner

A reliable manufacturing partner can guide you on fabric sourcing, suggest suitable alternatives and help you develop the right fabric for your target market.

At Teej, we support brands with fabric selection, sampling and full-scale production — helping turn an initial concept into a finished collection.
