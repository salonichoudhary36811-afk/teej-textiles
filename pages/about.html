Our Story

Built for the brands
we wished existed.

Teej India Textiles began in 2011 in Jaipur with a simple belief — manufacturing should feel less complicated for the brands behind every collection.

What started as a small apparel operation has grown into a trusted manufacturing partner for brands across the world, built on craftsmanship, long-term relationships and a commitment to getting the details right.

Today, we bring together design development, sourcing, sampling, production and export under one roof, helping brands of all sizes create clothing that people love to wear.
